OurAirports open data
=====================

Source: https://ourairports.com/data/
Repository: https://github.com/davidmegginson/ourairports-data

Terms of use, quoted from https://ourairports.com/data/ :

    All data is released to the Public Domain, and comes with no guarantee
    of accuracy or fitness for use.

The files vendored here (airports.csv.gz, runways.csv.gz) are gzip-compressed
copies of airports.csv and runways.csv with a subset of the original columns
retained; no values were altered.
