Ruby `mail` gem test fixtures
=============================

Source
    https://github.com/mikel/mail
    spec/fixtures/emails/  (error_emails, plain_emails, mime_emails,
                            attachment_emails, rfc2822, multi_charset,
                            multipart_report_emails, rfc6532)

All 103 .eml files here are byte-identical to those in the repository,
flattened into one directory. They are real messages collected as regression
tests for the gem: attachments with RFC 2231 and RFC 2047 filenames, nested
multiparts, 8-bit headers, and a good number of broken ones.
