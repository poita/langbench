Apache James mime4j test messages
=================================

Source
    https://github.com/apache/james-mime4j
    core/src/test/resources/mimetools-testmsgs/   (48 files)
    core/src/test/resources/testmsgs/             (32 files)

All 80 .msg files here are byte-identical to those in the repository; they are
the messages mime4j itself is tested against, which is why so many of them are
malformed on purpose (bad boundaries, truncated multiparts, obsolete header
syntax, unusual charsets).
