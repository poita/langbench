Corpus provenance and licence
=============================

The three text files in this directory are excerpts of public-domain works
obtained from Project Gutenberg (https://www.gutenberg.org/).  Each file
contains only the body of the work: the Project Gutenberg header, footer,
licence text, front matter and editorial apparatus have been removed, so the
files carry no Project Gutenberg trademark or licence obligations.  The
underlying works are in the public domain in the United States and in most
other jurisdictions because their authors died long before 1900.

  aesop.txt
      "Three Hundred Aesop's Fables", by Aesop, literally translated from the
      Greek by the Rev. George Fyler Townsend (d. 1900).
      Project Gutenberg eBook #21 (https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/21).
      Extract: the 313 fables, from the "AESOP'S FABLES" heading to the
      "FOOTNOTES" section.

  bacon.txt
      "The Essays or Counsels, Civil and Moral", by Francis Bacon (d. 1626).
      Project Gutenberg eBook #575 (https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/575).
      Extract: the 59 essays, from "Of Truth" to "Of Fame", without the
      glossary of archaic words.

  federalist.txt
      "The Federalist Papers", by Alexander Hamilton, John Jay and James
      Madison (published 1787-1788).
      Project Gutenberg eBook #1404 (https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1404).
      Extract: the 85 papers, from "FEDERALIST No. 1" to the end.

gen_tests.py converts these texts into the Markdown content of the
corresponding test sites.  Project Gutenberg's own note applies to the
originals: "This eBook is for the use of anyone anywhere in the United States
and most other parts of the world at no cost and with almost no restrictions
whatsoever."
